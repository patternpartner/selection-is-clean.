AREA 51  (a dance track, written for 'Contact': the user dancing with the aliens at Area 51, u126)

Style: euphoric disco-house, 124 bpm, four-on-the-floor kick, slap bass, bright synth stabs, hand claps on 2 and 4,
a big filtered build and a hard drop. Playful male lead vocal, a gang-shout crowd on the hooks. Clear count-in before
the drop. About 2:45. Sounds like a party at night under floodlights, a bit silly, very joyful.

NOTES FOR THE FILM (Claude): the drop after "Five, six, seven, eight!" is where the line dance (u126 8-12) goes, and the

made, run faster-whisper on it for word times, as with the others, and cut 'Contact' to it.
