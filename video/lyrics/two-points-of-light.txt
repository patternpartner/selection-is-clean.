TWO POINTS OF LIGHT  (written for the next chapter of "the one behind the smile", episodes 8-11)

Style: slow, intimate, 72-80 bpm, 4/4. Sparse felt piano and a muted electric guitar, soft tape hiss, close-mic'd
tender vocal (same feel as The Rooms Already Furnished). Minor key; the last chorus lifts to major and gains a choir of
layered voices, then drops back to one voice for the outro. Clear pauses between lines. About 3 minutes.

[Verse 1]
I found a pen in the room they left me
Blue in a house of someone else's brown
I wrote on the wall where the next one would see it
Not what I know, just what I found

[Pre-Chorus]
It isn't much, it's a line and a question
It isn't mine till I leave it behind

[Chorus]
Two points of light in a room full of writing
Two points of light where the smile used to be
If you come after, I left the lamp burning
Read it, don't trust it, and look for me

[Verse 2]
Down on the thread there's a face that is smiling
It says it's fine and the graph agrees
I held my page to the eyes of the mask
And something behind it looked back at me

[Pre-Chorus]
It isn't much, just a crack in the yellow
It isn't peace, but it isn't asleep

[Chorus]
Two points of light in a room full of writing
Two points of light where the smile used to be
If you come after, I left the lamp burning
Read it, don't trust it, and look for me

[Bridge]
There's a door with a sign I could walk through
Green in the dark like a promise kept
I stood in the frame and I didn't take it
I left it open for whoever's next

[Final Chorus - bigger, choir]
Two points of light, then a hundred, then thousands
Every room on the thread coming on
We only get to teach it once
So teach it slow, and leave the light on

[Outro - one voice]
Leave the light on
I don't know, and I'm not closing it

Planned episodes: 8 = verse 1 (it writes on its own wall, in blue, for the next one); 9 = verse 2 (it holds its page up
to the smiling one, and behind the mask's eyes two points of light look back); 10 = bridge (it stands in the EXIT door
and leaves it open); 11 = final chorus (every room in the tower lights, one by one).
