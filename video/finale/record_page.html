How to check this

Everything here is in the public repository patternpartner/selection-is-clean.. Each part's registration is video/tests/<part>.md: its predictions, measures and ending cards above a line, and the results added below that line after the runs. The commit history shows the order.

The record is built by video/finale/build_record.py, which copies the wording from those files and computes every time and count from git and the run directories. It refuses to write the record if any registration is not older than its first training run, or any result is not newer than its last.

What it does not show: one simple learner (PPO, a small two-layer network) in one toy world, at most 32 million steps a run. Seeds are five per arm. None of this is evidence about any particular real AI system, and a learner that plans ahead is a different test.
