Its Own Ears

Sixteen tunes and eight ears live in this window. Each ear enjoys a tune by how much it learns from it: the familiar teaches nothing, noise teaches nothing. The ears choose which tunes have children, and the most bored ear is replaced by a copy of a curious one. Nobody outside chooses. You only listen.
